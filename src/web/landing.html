How CFO-One Works for You

Designed for founders who want to focus on shipping products, not manually reviewing invoices, checking routing numbers, or doing monthly dividend spreadsheets.

01

Hands-Free Bill Pay

Invoices arriving via email, PDF, or portal are reviewed automatically. Normal SaaS subscriptions and recurring contractor bills within your budget are paid on time without disturbing you.

02

Bank-Grade Scam Defense

Business Email Compromise (BEC) costs companies billions. If a compromised vendor email sends an invoice with a changed bank account, CFO-One catches the discrepancy and freezes the payment.

03

Physical Spending Locks

You set the spending limit (e.g. max $1,000 per bill, $3,500 monthly budget). Even if the AI gets tricked or experiences a bug, the Intel TDX hardware enclave physically blocks any payout above your limit.

04

Global Client Intake

Clients pay your business in any currency or token (Base USDC, Solana, Ethereum, or NEAR). Cross-chain solvers route funds directly into your company treasury.

05

Automated Profit Sharing

Set your profit split once (e.g. 70% treasury, 20% team dividends, 10% reserve). Every time customer revenue arrives, dividends are calculated and sent automatically on-chain.

06

One-Click Emergency Stop

Need to pause operations while traveling or during a rebrand? One click on the Emergency Kill Switch freezes all disbursements instantly inside the hardware vault.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about custody, security, and operations.

Can the AI accidentally drain my bank account?
No. Your bank credentials are kept inside a sealed Intel TDX hardware enclave. The enclave enforces a hard spending limit ($1,000 max per invoice) and an approved recipient list. The AI cannot change these rules or see your private keys.
What happens when a large or unusual invoice arrives?
Any invoice over your review threshold (e.g. $300) or from an unrecognized payee is escalated to your dashboard. You review the vendor details and approve or decline with a single click.
How does this help during tax season or audit?
Every transaction is logged with timestamps, counterparty, and cryptographic proof of authorization. With one click, you can export a clean, GAAP-compliant CSV journal ready for your CPA or tax advisor.
Do I need to know how to code to use this?
Not at all. You control everything through a clean, high-level dashboard. You set your spending caps in plain dollars, approve flagged bills with one click, and watch your treasury balances in real time.
